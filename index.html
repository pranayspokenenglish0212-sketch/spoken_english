About Our Classes

At Pranay Spoken English & Personality Development Classes, we simplify the journey to fluent communication. Instead of making you memorize complex grammar frameworks, we utilize intuitive conversation techniques, practical situational practices, and real-time feedback loops to transform how you speak.

Our focus is on unlocking natural dialogue, reducing performance anxiety, and building a conversational personality that commands respect in boardrooms, colleges, and social gatherings alike.
